refactor: extract network-driven file sharing (Step 13c)

Step 13c of the refactor plan. The File Sharing section's
network-driven methods are extracted to node-service-fileshare.ts
behind a `FileShareNetworkContext` interface.

13c extracts 3 public methods + 2 private helpers:
  - discoverPublishedLibraryViaRuntime
  - shareFileViaRuntime
  - requestShareFromLibraryViaRuntime
  - (the private _shareFileInternal / _requestShareFromLibraryInternal
    bodies moved into the runtime; the old private methods are
    deleted from the class)
  - the call sites of _shareFileInternal (shareChatFileToMember
    adapter and sendChatRoomAttachment) are rewired to call
    shareFileViaRuntime directly

FileShareNetworkContext extends FileShareContext with:
  - assertOnline, requireMesh, requireProfile (mesh+profile gates)
  - resolvePeerTransportForOwner, dialHintsForChat (transport)
  - getBonds (filter to non-blocked)
  - deliverCallEnvelope (mesh dispatch)
  - setPendingPushShare / setPendingPullShare / setCorrelationByRequestMsgId
    (writes to the private internal maps)
  - upsertTransferStatus (status-log append)
  - isVaultPathSafe, assertVaultFileExists (vault-path safety checks)

The two callers of the now-deleted _shareFileInternal are
rewired in this commit; both still go through the same audit-event
+ pending-share + transfer-status recording (now via the runtime).
